Yogabar · ITC-owned healthy snacking · Sep 2025 – present

A repeat-revenue engine from zero that ended up redirecting company strategy

Yogabar was growing almost entirely on acquisition: no lifecycle journeys, no broadcast programme, no segmentation. I was engaged to build the retention charter from scratch: strategy, infrastructure, and execution.

The build
  • Lifecycle journeys shipped in month one; the daily broadcast programme in month two, at 8.7 RoAS in its first month.
  • Segmentation evolved to recency × product × acquisition-year, and spend moved 100% to cohorts that paid back.
  • A narrative system (reorder, cross-sell, engagement, offer, wallet hooks) tracked on per-narrative ROI; the wallet-expiry trigger became the programme's best campaign at RoAS 56.
The experiment that changed strategy

Mid-November tests on High Protein Oats cohorts converted at 3 to 4 times the account average, and cohort data showed oats buyers repeating fastest at M0–M1. I recommended shifting hero-product focus from RTD protein shakes (high AOV, low repeat) to oats (low AOV, high frequency), trading ticket size for lifetime value. The team adopted it: oats became the lead acquisition and CRM product, and December oats campaigns ran at ROI 7 to 8.

moha & Vedistry · Charak Pharma D2C · Dec 2025 – present

A content-intelligence engine for two-brand retention

Two ayurvedic D2C brands running WhatsApp and SMS CRM across lifecycle flows and daily campaigns. I own retention strategy, the content system, experimentation, and monthly executive reporting.

2.4×click-to-order from pattern-based copy rewrites
  • The divergence: moha's store contracted, with monthly orders falling to roughly a third of where they began, while its repeat share climbed to a series-high 57.5%. Vedistry built repeat revenue about fivefold on a growing base. One engine, two starting points.
  • Content analysis: 39 live recovery copies distilled into six winning patterns and the anti-patterns behind fifteen zero-converting sends. The proof pairs: the same SMS slot at 10.95× ROI vs 0×, and one identical creative at 1.65× vs 15.15× under two different copies. In WhatsApp recovery, copy converts; the creative amplifies.
  • A/B testing: copy-isolated tests (equal splits, identical landing page) put rewrites at 2.4× the incumbent's click-to-order while the incumbent won raw clicks. Curiosity clicks are not intent clicks.
  • Flows over broadcasts: triggered automations out-returned calendar broadcasts on every efficiency metric; Vedistry automations returned ₹3.96 per rupee against ₹1.04 from broadcasts.
  • System: the loop runs on an LLM-assisted pipeline I designed: campaign exports in; diagnosis, corrected copy, and re-tests out, every month.
